📖 Accessible Guide: How HarmonyCube Works

A step-by-step, plain-language guide showcasing the bidirectional translation and negotiation process between autistic children and family members.

1

Child Squeezes Pad (Face 1)

During sensory overload or stress, the student simply squeezes the tactile pad (Face 1). No spoken words required.

Action: Squeeze force conveys overload level from 0% (calm) to 100% (urgent break needed).
2

Translation to Parent Text (Face 2 ➔ Face 3)

Face 2 converts the physical squeeze into clear, supportive text for the parent's device (*"I am overwhelmed and need a break"*).

Action: Parent sees quantified distress without misunderstanding or guessing.
3

Parent Text Converts to Child Vibration (Face 3 ➔ Face 2)

When the parent sends a text message, Face 2 translates the text into gentle haptic vibration patterns on the child's cube.

Action: The child physically feels reassurance (e.g. gentle double pulse) without auditory pressure.
4

AI Clinician Mediation (Face 4)

The Harmony Negotiation Side (Face 4) balances the student's distress with the learning goals, proposing 3 fair compromise choices.

Action: Both sides agree on a compromise and initiate the coping break.
