📖 Accessible Guide: How HarmonyCube Works

A step-by-step, plain-language guide for autistic individuals, parents, teachers, and clinicians to achieve communication harmony without verbal stress.

1

Express Without Words

During sensory overload or stress, talking can be exhausting. The student simply squeezes the tactile pad (Face 1) or uses a gentle gesture (Face 2) to show how overwhelmed they feel.

Action: Squeeze the pad to show distress level from 0% (calm) to 100% (urgent break needed).
2

Parent Sends Gentle Cues

Parents or aides type supportive messages or choose quick empathetic chips on Face 3, avoiding high-pressure spoken demands.

Action: Send suggestions like "Try headphones first?" or "Finish 2 problems then quiet break?"
3

AI & Clinician Mediate

The Harmony Engine (Face 4) blends the student's sensory urgency with the learning goals to suggest 3 fair compromise choices based on occupational therapy protocols.

Action: Select a compromise that both child and parent feel comfortable with.
4

Sensory Co-Regulation

Once agreed, the cube pulses with a calming breathing light (Face 5) and soothing harmonic tones to help everyone reset together peacefully.

Action: Breathe with the glowing orb (4-7-8 rhythm) as the coping break begins.
